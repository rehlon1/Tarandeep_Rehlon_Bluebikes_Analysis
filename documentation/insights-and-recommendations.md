# Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Demand growth

Annual journey demand increased from approximately **1.24M journeys in 2016** to **2.52M in 2019**, representing approximately **104.1% growth**.

The monthly pattern is strongly seasonal: winter demand is substantially lower than warmer-season demand.

### Recommendation

Do not treat the growth as one uniform network-wide increase. Plan capacity and operations by **district, season and station**.

## 2. User behaviour

Subscribers account for approximately **80.6%** of journeys.

Subscriber journeys have clear peaks around **08:00 and 17:00**, while Customer demand is flatter across the day and relatively stronger at weekends.

Median journey duration:
- Subscribers: **~10.1 minutes**
- Customers: **~21.2 minutes**

### Recommendation

Separate commuter-style and leisure-style demand in operational planning rather than treating all users as one demand profile.

## 3. Station demand and capacity pressure

Journey starts are concentrated around transport, university and employment hubs.

South Station, Central Square and MIT Stata Center are among the high-demand locations.

Starts per dock provides a relative indication of demand pressure against stated station capacity.

### Recommendation

Review stations where **high starts per dock** and persistent imbalance overlap before considering uniform capacity expansion.

## 4. Directional imbalance

Net flow is defined as:

```text
Total Ends - Total Starts
```

Positive values indicate accumulation pressure; negative values indicate depletion pressure.

Examples:
- Nashua Street at Red Auerbach Way: **~+28,646**
- MIT Stata Center: **~+16,864**
- 359 Broadway: **~-8,601**

### Recommendation

Use persistent station-flow patterns to move bikes from accumulation locations toward depletion locations **before** the strongest weekday demand peaks.

## 5. Weather and demand

Average daily demand declines with heavier rainfall:

- Dry: **~1,303**
- Light rain: **~1,251**
- Moderate rain: **~1,158**
- Heavy rain: **~811**

Snowfall days average approximately **532 journeys**, versus approximately **1,579** when no snowfall is recorded.

Warmer conditions are generally associated with higher demand.

### Recommendation

Use short-term weather forecasts alongside historical seasonal patterns to adjust:

- redistribution
- staffing
- maintenance scheduling

## Priority rule

Act first where the following overlap:

**high demand + constrained capacity + directional imbalance + time sensitivity**

This focuses operational effort where it is most likely to improve bike and dock availability.
